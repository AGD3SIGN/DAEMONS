# Demo singles: Captchat, Phishgill, Cumulonimbyte

Nos. 023–025, added 2026-11-26 for the demo roster. Paste one prompt per image. Drop the result in
art_src/ as `<id>_gpt_v1.png` (captchat, phishgill, cumulonimbyte) and run the sprite import. Until then the
game draws a placeholder blob for each.

## Captchat

No. 023 · Security · single stage

````
Create ONE image: a pixel-art battle sprite sheet for ONE creature from my original indie game HEXFORGED
(a monster-collecting RPG where technology comes alive: every creature is a real animal fused with
real IT hardware).

== CANVAS AND GRID ==
- Square image, 1024 x 1024 pixels.
- An invisible 4 x 4 grid: 16 cells, each 256 x 256. Do NOT draw grid lines, borders, boxes, panels,
  frames, labels, numbers or any text anywhere in the image.
- Exactly one sprite per cell, centered horizontally. In every cell the feet rest on the same baseline, 16 pixels above the bottom of the cell. Nothing crosses into a
  neighboring cell.
- Background: one flat, solid magenta #FF00FF filling the whole image, including the space between
  sprites. No floor, no cast shadow, no scenery, no gradient, no vignette.

== PIXEL STYLE ==
- Early-2000s handheld RPG battle sprite (Game Boy Advance era).
- Each sprite is drawn on a 64 x 64 art-pixel grid and scaled up exactly 4x, so every art pixel is a
  crisp 4 x 4 square. The pixel size is identical in all 16 cells.
- Hard edges only: no anti-aliasing, blur, smooth shading, gradients, glow, bloom, noise or texture.
- A 1-art-pixel outline around the creature in a dark tint of its body color, not pure black.
- Cel shading: base color, one shadow, one highlight per material. Light comes from the top-left.
- Maximum 15 colors: use ONLY the palette below, identical in every cell.
- Effects (sparks, impact stars, rings, Zz, steam) are drawn in the same pixel style and palette.
- 100% original: it must not resemble any existing game, cartoon or mascot character.

== CREATURE BIBLE ==
Name: Captchat. Single stage (does not evolve). Type: Security.
Concept: a sleek, suspicious cat that guards login pages and makes everyone prove they're human. Smug, judgmental, secretly unsure it would pass its own test.
Body: a slim sitting cat with an upright tail and pointed ears.
Size: body height about 50% of the cell (about 32 of the 64 art pixels), same size in every frame.
Signature features (show in every frame where they would be visible):
1. A glowing checkbox on its chest, empty in most frames and ticked in the Happy frame.
2. Ears shaped like little shield badges with a keyhole dot.
3. A tail that ends in a small 3x3 grid of picture squares.
4. A collar tag with a few squiggly, unreadable marks (not real letters).
5. Narrow green eyes with vertical pupils and one white shine pixel.
Palette (exact hex):
#1E2430 outline | #5A6F8C slate fur | #3E4E66 fur shadow | #8AA0BC fur highlight |
#E8EEF4 chest white | #46C2B8 checkbox teal | #2E8A82 checkbox shadow | #F2C63A tag gold |
#6CD86C eye green | #141418 pupil | #FFFFFF eye shine | #E88A9A nose pink |
#C8D0DC grid light | #8B6FD6 grid violet | #E84848 error red

== FRAMES (read left to right, top to bottom) ==
Row 1 - FRONT view (facing the viewer, body turned slightly toward the viewer's left):
 1. Idle A: sitting tall, tail curled, one eyebrow raised.
 2. Idle B: identical to Idle A but 1 art pixel lower and slightly squashed wider (breathing).
 3. Physical attack: a paw swipe toward the viewer's left, a small checkbox-shaped spark at the claws.
 4. Special attack: tail grid held up, a fan of small squiggly picture squares flying toward the viewer's left.
Row 2 - FRONT reactions:
 5. Hurt: recoiling to the right, eyes squeezed shut (> <), small white impact star at its upper-left.
 6. Fainted: lying flat on its side, eyes as X shapes, checkbox dark.
 7. Happy / Victory: sitting proudly, eyes closed in happy arcs, the chest checkbox ticked.
 8. Asleep (Standby): curled into a loaf, eyes closed as flat lines, small pixel "Z z".
Row 3 - BACK view (seen from behind, facing away toward the upper right):
 9. Back Idle A: sitting, tail grid visible, ears up.
10. Back Idle B: 1 art pixel lower, slightly squashed.
11. Back attack: pouncing forward away from the viewer toward the upper right.
12. Back hurt: flinching back toward the viewer (lower-left).
Row 4 - SPECIAL (front view):
13. Signature pose: one paw raised like a stop sign, checkbox glowing, tail grid fanned out.
14. Overheated: flustered, sweat drop, small white steam puffs rising off its ears.
15. Throttled: stiff frozen pose, eyes wide, small zig-zag sparks around the body.
16. Menu icon: head and upper body only, front view, simplified, drawn within a 32 x 32 art-pixel area
    centered in the cell (same 4x pixel size as the other cells).

== CONSISTENCY CHECKLIST (check before finishing) ==
- The same creature in all 16 cells: same proportions, eyes, palette and pixel size; only the pose and
  effects change.
- Chest checkbox, shield-badge ears and picture-grid tail wherever visible.
- Feet on the same baseline in every cell except Fainted and the Happy hop.
- No text, labels, numbers, grid lines, borders or extra characters. Flat #FF00FF background everywhere.
````
